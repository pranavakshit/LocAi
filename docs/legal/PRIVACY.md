# Privacy Policy

## The Human Version
We believe your data belongs to you. LocAi is built to be a local-first application, which means we work hard to keep as much of your information right where it belongs: on your own computer.

Here is a simple breakdown of what data stays with you, and what data leaves your device (and why).

**1. What Stays on Your Device (100% Private)**
By default, **LocAi is fully local**. The following data never leaves your computer unless you explicitly choose to send it somewhere else:
- **Your Code and Files:** Any files you open, edit, or analyze with LocAi.
- **Your Chat History:** Every conversation you have with a local AI model is stored directly on your hard drive.
- **Your Local Settings:** Your custom prompts, preferences, and project configurations.
- **Local AI Processing:** When you use downloaded local models (like Llama or Gemma), the AI thinks and generates its responses entirely on your machine. We do not see your prompts or the AI's answers.

**2. What Leaves Your Device (When Using Online Features)**
LocAi does have some online features to make your experience better. If you consent to use them, here is exactly what data leaves your device and where it goes:

*When Checking for Updates:*
- **What goes:** Your current LocAi version number and your operating system (e.g., Windows).
- **Where it goes:** Our secure update servers.
- **Why:** So we can tell you if there's a newer, better version of LocAi available to download.

*When Downloading Local Models:*
- **What goes:** The name of the model you want to download and your IP address (so the server can send the file back to you).
- **Where it goes:** To the model hosting service (like HuggingFace or Ollama).
- **Why:** So your computer can download the requested AI model.

*When Using Cloud AI Models (Optional):*
*Note: This only happens if you specifically connect a third-party API key (like OpenAI or Anthropic).*
- **What goes:** The specific chat messages and code snippets you include in that conversation, plus your API key.
- **Where it goes:** Directly to the cloud provider you selected (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google).
- **Why:** Because their servers need your prompt to generate a response.
- **What you should know:** We don't intercept this data. It goes straight from your app to the provider. You should read their privacy policies to understand how they handle your data.

*When Using Git Features (Optional):*
- **What goes:** Your git commands (commits, pushes) are sent to your configured git remote (like GitHub or GitLab).
- **Where it goes:** To your repository hosting provider.
- **Why:** To back up your code and collaborate with others.

**3. We Don't Sell Your Data**
We do not sell your personal data. We don't collect secret telemetry, and we don't spy on your local code. We only collect the bare minimum needed to make the online features work.

**4. You Are In Control**
If you don't like any of the online features mentioned above, you can decline them. If you decline, LocAi will run in a strict "offline-only" mode. You won't get automatic updates and you won't be able to download new models through the app, but everything else will continue to work perfectly using your existing local resources.

## The Legal Version
This Privacy Policy explains how LocAi ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the LocAi application ("Software").

### 1. Information Collection
**Local Processing:** By default, LocAi operates locally on your machine. Any code, data, or chats processed using local AI models do not leave your device unless explicitly configured to do so by the User.
**Online Features:** If you enable online capabilities (such as updates, telemetry, or integration with external cloud APIs), we or our third-party partners may collect metadata, crash reports, IP addresses, and configuration data strictly necessary to provide these services.

### 2. Use of Information
Any information collected through online features is used exclusively to:
- Deliver updates and maintain application stability.
- Route inference requests to cloud providers (if configured by the User).
- Improve the functionality and user experience of LocAi.

### 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. Data transmitted for online features is shared only with the specific third-party providers required to fulfill those requests (e.g., Anthropic, OpenAI, GitHub), and is subject to their respective privacy policies. We are not liable for the data handling practices of these third-party providers.

### 4. Consent and Control
The User retains full control over online features. If the User declines this Privacy Policy or disables online capabilities in the settings, the Software will operate in a strictly offline mode, and no diagnostic or feature-related data will be transmitted.

### 5. Updates to this Policy
We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy as new features are added. The User will be notified of significant changes via an in-app prompt and must consent to the updated policy to continue using online features.
